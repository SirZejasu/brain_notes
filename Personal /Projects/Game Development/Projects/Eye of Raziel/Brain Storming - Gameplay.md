# Drawing Constellations and creating runes
>[!note] Idea
> By drawing runes / constellation, you not only create spells but magical laws.
> Or just laws?
> You can spells with laws?


In the game you are able to connect stars. 
You are doing that to create runes. Symbols to manifest spells.

You will find informations and theories about runes you made and their attributes. These informations will tell you with which [[Celestial Bodies]] they are associated with.

## Steps to create a spell / magical law
>[!question] Randideen / Fragen  
> Ist die Reihenfolge der [[Celestial Bodies]] wichtig? 
> Eigenes Bild zeichnen für die Konstellation, was dann im Hintergrund der Konstellation erscheint?


1. Find out what kind of spell you want. Experiment with elements/attributes. With enough knowledge and work, you will create "Theoretical spells".
	1. The Theoretical Spell looks like that:
		1. (X-Amount) of [[Celestial Bodies|CB]]-Type
		2. (X-Amount) of [[Celestial Bodies|CB]]-Type
		3. ...
2. Finde diese Sterne mithilfe deiner aufgezeichneten Sternenkarte
3. Verbinde die passenden Sterne und erstelle eine Rune.
## 1 Soduko Like
- Es können nicht 2 gleiche Sterne im selben Feld, Reihe und Spalte sein.

### Limitation
Limitationen können erniedrigt werden während des Fortschritts im Spiel.
- 1. Nur benachbarte Sterne in bestimmter Distanz können verbunden werden (Das werde ich nicht ändern, das wäre albern)
- 2. Es können nicht unendlich viele Sterne verbunden werden. Die Anzahl der Kombination ist limitiert -> Sollte am Ende nicht allzu stark erhöht werden.

# The Tower
As a lonely academic you begin in an empty tower. You chose this place so you can go on with your life and study your field in peace. It is not like you hate people. You even made a few friends outside the tower. However, as you grew older, you start to appreciate the peace and silence of solitude.
This is where it will start. You will rewrite reality and thus define the fate of the world. It is inevitable. 
Chose wisely. Your actions will have consequences. As the first wizard/witch/magical being you will define magic in this world. You can't control on how it will be used but you might control its behavior.
And remember...
They will observe you.
