This spell is giving you knowledge of that what can't be comprehended.
This is where you start to understand, what "Magic" is. And it is very much real. Well, sort of.

Magic is in words and runes. Words have influence and power. They summon emotions or cast them out. There are invisible forces not inside the universe as we know it. But also not really outside of the universe. There is no 'outside'. The universe is everything that is. There is no 'nothing'. All is, what is. All what is not, does not and can not exist.

The forces, however, barely exist.

These forces are what bind what is. They bind all what can be comprehended.  These forces are the absolute invisible relationship between those things. They are thoughts. They are visions. They are Not only invisible by light, but by anything. They has no gravity, no density, no energy, not even a sound of a beating heart. Nothing. Not that you would hear that through the vacuum of the cosmos anyways. 
But they are there. And somehow, you can reach them. You can manipulate them through... [[Eye of Azriel|the artifact]].
And through these relationships that you create with these forces, something new can be created. Something that does not have to obey the laws of the universe. This is magic.

This is the moment where the protagonist accepts the existence of magic. The first magic user is born.
