You play a wizard hero to defeat the "evil" witch that trapped the world into eternal nighttime.
Master the 5 Elements to strategically defeat your enemies. 
Each fullmoon you gain the ability to change your magical abilities and turn battles to your favor, if you successfully draw the constellations in the sky that is.

After events of "Eye Of Azriel"

Dungeon Run:
- 4 Spell slots -> At specific events (Full moon event) you can change those Spells to fit the current situation
- Quick Time Events -> Draw constellations with specific symbols under time pressure
- You play as a wizard 
- Type System: Rock-Paper-Scissor-Logic
